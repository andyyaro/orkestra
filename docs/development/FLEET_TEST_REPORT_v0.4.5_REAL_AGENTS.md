One `orkestra run` in the background took ~19.5 minutes wall clock end-to-end (started 16:00:36, complete by ~16:20:30) and reported `53k tokens · $2.82`. Phase pacing was very uneven for a newcomer: ~2 min of analysis, ~5 min stuck in `planning` with an empty task table and no elapsed-time or phase-progress hint, then ~12 min of task execution. The plan was genuinely cross-challenged - codex and antigravity each returned "revise (5 concerns)" - and two of three tasks got an explicit independent review event ("review by codex: approved", "review by claude: approved").
